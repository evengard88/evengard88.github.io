How It Works

1

Upload a Photo

Take a photo of your snake next to a reference object like a ruler or credit card.

2

Set the Reference

Mark two points on the reference object and enter its known size.

3

Measure the Snake

Trace points along your snake's body. The app calculates the real length instantly.
