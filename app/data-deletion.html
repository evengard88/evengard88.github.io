Deleting Your Account and Data

Applies to both Snake Measurer (snakemeasurer.com) and Scalebook (scalebook.app). They share one account, so deleting it removes your data from both.

1. What the account holds

  • Your email address and the sign-in method you used.
  • Measurements, including the photographs they were taken from.
  • Animal profiles, their gallery photographs, and the species you chose.
  • Diary entries, schedules, state spans and enclosures.
  • Your subscription status, and a Telegram identifier if you linked one.

2. Deleting it yourself

Open Settings → Delete account in Snake Measurer. This removes the account and everything listed above.

3. Deleting it by email

If you cannot open the app, write to support@snakemeasurer.com from the email address on the account and ask for deletion. We ask from that address because it is the only way we can tell it is yours — we will not delete an account on the word of someone who cannot prove it.

4. What happens, and when

  • Records, photographs and profiles are permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.
  • Photographs are removed from our image storage in the same pass.
  • Records of payments are kept where bookkeeping law requires it. They contain the fact and amount of a purchase, not your animals or your photographs.
  • Backups roll over on their own schedule, so a copy may persist in an encrypted backup for up to 30 further days before it is overwritten. It is not readable or searchable in the meantime.

5. Diaries kept without a name

Scalebook can be used without creating a named account. We hold no email address or name for such a diary, which has one consequence worth stating plainly: if you have lost access to it, we cannot identify it in order to delete it. There is nothing we can match your request against. While you still have access, signing in attaches your name to that same diary, after which it can be deleted like any other account.

6. Deleting one thing instead of everything

You do not have to delete the account to remove data from it. Any measurement, entry, photograph or animal can be deleted on its own, on any plan, free of charge — and so can a whole animal together with its history.
